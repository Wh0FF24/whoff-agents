Questions couples ask

How do I make a wedding seating chart?

Paste your guest list, add tables and drag them to match your venue, then tap tables to seat guests. When everyone has a seat, print a per-table chart plus an alphabetical lookup. Most couples finish a 100-guest chart in under 30 minutes.

Is SeatSage free?

Yes. The free plan covers up to 3 tables and 25 guests with printing, autosave and backups. A one-time $14.99 Pro upgrade unlocks unlimited tables and guests plus PNG and CSV export. There is no subscription.

Can anyone see my guest list?

No. SeatSage runs entirely in your browser and your guest list is stored only on your device. There is no server copy and no account.

Can I print the seating chart?

Yes. One click prints a seating chart grouped by table, an alphabetical find-your-seat list, and your floor plan, formatted for paper.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. SeatSage works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and laptops, and you can install it to your home screen. It keeps working offline at the venue.

What happens if I lose my work?

SeatSage autosaves on every change, and you can download a backup file at any time and restore it on any device.
